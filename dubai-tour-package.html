Day-by-Day Experience

Detailed 06 Days / 05 Nights Tour Itinerary

Carefully structured timeline ensuring a relaxed, seamless journey without feeling rushed.

01

Welcome to Dubai • Private Airport Transfer • Marina Dhow Cruise

Arrival Day • Private Luxury Chauffeur
PVT Airport Transfer Dhow Cruise Dinner

Welcome to the United Arab Emirates!

  • Touch down at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Warm Meet & Greet by Aksh Tours & Travels private chauffeur.
  • Transfer on Private Basis directly to your hotel. Seamless check-in (Standard check-in time: 14:00 Hrs). Unwind and refresh.
  • Evening: Private transfer to Dubai Marina for the Traditional Wooden Dhow Cruise.
  • Enjoy a lavish 2-hour illuminated cruise gliding past Dubai Marina's world-famous skyscrapers, complete with international buffet dinner, tanoura folk dance, and live music. Return to hotel.
02

Half-Day Dubai City Tour • Dubai Frame • Indian Lunch • Burj Khalifa 124th Floor

Certified Guide • Dubai Mall & Musical Fountain
Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner PVT Transfers
  • Relish a hearty buffet breakfast at your hotel.
  • Embark on a guided Half-Day Dubai City Tour with professional guide. Enjoy curated photo-stops at Burj Al Arab, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Mosque, Atlantis The Palm, Jumeirah Beach, and Museum of the Future.
  • Visit the world-famous Dubai Frame — walk the transparent glass sky bridge overlooking Old & New Dubai.
  • Savor delicious Lunch @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) with private transfers.
  • Afternoon/Evening: Visit Dubai Mall and ascend to At The Top Burj Khalifa (124th & 125th floor) - Non-Prime Hours. Witness 360-degree panoramic views of Dubai's skyline and ocean.
  • Watch the spellbinding Dubai Musical Fountain Show along the Burj Khalifa lake and explore the Dubai Aquarium exterior.
  • Enjoy Dinner @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) with private transfer back to hotel.
03

Leisure / Souk Shopping • Indian Lunch • 4x4 Desert Safari & BBQ Dinner

Red Dunes • Dune Bashing • Camel Ride • Tanoura & Fire Show
Breakfast + Lunch + BBQ Dinner PVT Transfers
  • Hotel breakfast. Morning free for leisure or shopping at the world-renowned Gold Souk & Spice Souk in Deira.
  • Delicious Lunch @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) with private transfers.
  • Afternoon: Pick up in private 4x4 Land Cruiser for the iconic Lahbab Red Dune Desert Safari.
  • Heart-pumping 4x4 Dune Bashing with licensed safari drivers, sandboarding down golden slopes, and sunset photoshoot on the dunes.
  • Arrive at traditional Bedouin Camp: enjoy camel rides, henna painting, Arabic coffee & dates.
  • Relish a lavish BBQ Dinner (Jain, vegetarian, and non-veg options) accompanied by thrilling Fire Shows, Belly Dance, and Tanoura performances under starry Arabian skies. Return to hotel.
04

Palm Monorail • Indian Lunch • Miracle Garden • Global Village

150M+ Flowers • 90+ Country Pavilions
Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner PVT Transfers
  • Morning breakfast at hotel.
  • Experience the panoramic Palm Jumeirah Monorail ride with breathtaking views of the Atlantis resort and Arabian Gulf (one-way ticket included).
  • Lunch @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) with private transfers.
  • Afternoon: Visit the world's largest natural floral paradise — Dubai Miracle Garden, showcasing over 150 million flowers arranged in stunning aircraft, castles, and heart arches (guide included).
  • Evening: Head to Global Village, the iconic multicultural festival destination featuring international shopping, cultural performances, and street food from 90+ nations.
  • Dinner @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) with private transfer to hotel.
05

Full Day Abu Dhabi Tour • Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque • BAPS Hindu Mandir • Ferrari World

UAE Capital Excursion • Certified Tour Guide
Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner PVT Transfers
  • Breakfast at hotel. Private morning departure to Abu Dhabi, the glamorous capital of the UAE.
  • Visit the architectural marvel Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque with our certified guide. Admire white marble domes, reflective pools, and the world's largest hand-knotted carpet *(AED 10 direct mosque counter entry permission)*.
  • Visit the newly opened, historic BAPS Hindu Mandir in Abu Dhabi — a spiritual masterpiece hand-sculpted by skilled artisans from pink sandstone and Italian Carrara marble.
  • Photo stop at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi & Yas Island.
  • Lunch @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) in Abu Dhabi.
  • Dinner @ Indian Restaurant (Thali Meals) before private return transfer to Dubai for overnight stay.
06

Hotel Check-Out • Private Airport Drop • Departure

End-to-End Service Completion by Aksh Tours
Breakfast at Hotel PVT Airport Transfer
  • Savor your final breakfast at the hotel.
  • Standard check-out from hotel (Check-out time: 12:00 Hrs).
  • Your Aksh Tours private chauffeur will transfer you comfortably to Dubai International Airport (DXB) in time for your flight.
  • Depart with golden memories of your Dubai & Abu Dhabi getaway — completed end-to-end with the care and excellence of Aksh Tours & Travels!
