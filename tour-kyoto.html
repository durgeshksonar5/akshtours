About Kyoto Heritage Journey

Step into the spiritual and aesthetic heart of traditional Japan with the Kyoto Ancient Temples & Zen Gardens tour crafted by Aksh Tours & Travels. As Japan’s imperial capital for over a millennium, Kyoto harbors over 2,000 Buddhist temples and Shinto shrines, pristine dry rock zen landscapes, and timeless cobblestone alleys.

Walk beneath the hypnotic tunnel of ten thousand vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Taisha, listen to the whisper of wind through towering stalks in the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, witness the radiant reflection of Kinkaku-ji (The Golden Pavilion) across Kyoko-chi mirror pond, and participate in a ceremonial Uji matcha tea ritual.

Schedule

Tour Itinerary

2 Days Itinerary

Arrival in Kyoto via scenic express train. Check into your tranquil accommodation with tatami styling. In the afternoon, explore Fushimi Inari Taisha, climbing through thousands of bright red torii paths. As dusk settles, enjoy a guided stroll through Gion's preserved wooden machiya merchant houses.

Morning visit to the magnificent Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), covered in delicate gold leaf. Experience the peaceful rock garden of Ryoan-ji. Afterwards, travel to Arashiyama to walk through towering bamboo groves and cross the historic Togetsukyo Bridge, followed by an authentic Zen tea ceremony.

Visit the dramatic hillside temple of Kiyomizu-dera, celebrated for its wooden stage offering panoramic Kyoto vistas without a single nail. Conclude your voyage exploring the vibrant flavors and crafts of Nishiki Market before your private Shinkansen bullet train departure.
