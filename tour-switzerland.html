Schedule

Tour Itinerary

4 Days / 5 Nights

Arrive at Zurich Airport and board your first-class panoramic train to picturesque Lucerne. Check into your lakeside hotel and stroll across the historic wooden Chapel Bridge. In the late afternoon, enjoy an enchanting sunset paddle steamboat cruise across Lake Lucerne framed by Mount Pilatus.

Journey via the Eiger Express gondola and historic Jungfrau cogwheel railway to the highest railway station in Europe at 3,454m. Stand on the Sphinx Observation Terrace looking out over the magnificent Aletsch Glacier—the longest glacier in the Alps—and walk through the shimmering glacial tunnels of the Ice Palace.

Travel into the car-free mountain haven of Zermatt. Ascend via 3S cable car to the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, the highest cable car station in Europe at 3,883m, with breathtaking 360-degree vistas of 38 alpine peaks exceeding 4,000 meters. Savor a traditional Swiss cheese fondue dinner.

Visit the fairytale valley of Lauterbrunnen, famed for its 72 cascading waterfalls including Staubbach Falls. Conclude with a scenic rail segment along the Glacier Express route, gazing out at alpine meadows, stone bridges, and crystal rivers before your return transfer to Zurich.
