About Paris, France

Paris, the globally celebrated capital of France, is an enduring beacon of romance, high fashion, fine art, and architectural splendor. Situated along the winding banks of the Seine River, the city offers an unparalleled tapestry of world-famous monuments, tree-lined boulevards, vibrant sidewalk cafés, and artistic heritage.

From ascending the wrought-iron summit of the iconic Eiffel Tower to contemplating masterpiece paintings inside the vast corridors of the Louvre Museum, every quarter of Paris tells an enchanting story.
