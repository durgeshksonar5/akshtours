About Phuket Island Discovery

Immerse yourself in Thailand’s coastal jewel with the Phuket Island & Coral Bay Discovery tour package curated by Aksh Tours & Travels. Fringed by turquoise Andaman waters, lush limestone cliffs, and tranquil palm-lined shores, this journey blends exhilarating speedboat adventures with serene beachfront rejuvenation.

Sail to the legendary Phi Phi Islands and Maya Bay, kayak through hidden lagoons in Phang Nga Bay, snorkel over multi-colored coral reefs teeming with marine life, and marvel at the 45-meter-high Big Buddha commanding sweeping panoramic vistas over Chalong Bay.

Schedule

Tour Itinerary

4 Days Plan

Arrive at Phuket International Airport where your private Aksh Tours & Travels chauffeur awaits with a warm welcome. Enjoy scenic coastal transfer to your 4-star beachfront resort. Spend the afternoon settling into your oceanfront room, followed by an evening stroll along Patong or Kata Beach with a welcoming welcome drink.

Board a premium high-speed boat to the legendary Phi Phi archipelago. Explore the stunning turquoise cove of Maya Bay, made famous worldwide. Dive into warm crystalline waters at Coral Bay and Shark Point to snorkel along vibrant coral gardens, followed by a beachfront buffet lunch on Phi Phi Don Island.

Explore the breathtaking limestone monoliths of Phang Nga Bay. Glide via guided canoes into secret sea cave lagoons and visit the famed James Bond Island. In the afternoon, visit the monumental 45-meter Big Buddha statue perched atop Nakkerd Hills, offering 360-degree views over Phuket Island.

Relish a leisurely tropical breakfast overlooking the ocean. Explore the colorful Sino-Portuguese heritage architecture of Phuket Old Town for authentic souvenirs and artisanal Thai silk. Private chauffeur transfer to Phuket Airport for your homeward journey with cherished memories.
