About Dubai Safari & City Wonders

Witness the unforgettable juxtaposition of high-octane desert dunes and futuristic sky-piercing architectural wonders with the Dunes & Desert Safari Tracks package curated by Aksh Tours & Travels. Discover why Dubai is the world’s ultimate luxury playground.

Climb into a 4x4 Land Cruiser for exhilarating dune bashing across crimson desert ridges, glide down velvet sand dunes on sandboards, and witness a majestic sunset over camel caravans. At dusk, unwind in an atmospheric Bedouin oasis camp featuring gourmet BBQ feast, fire dancers, and Tanoura performances beneath a canopy of desert stars.

Schedule

Tour Itinerary

3 Days / 4 Nights

Touch down in Dubai where your dedicated Aksh Tours & Travels chauffeur provides VIP limousine transfer to your 5-star hotel. In the evening, step aboard a traditional wooden Dhow for a 2-hour illuminated dinner cruise along Dubai Marina’s skyline, with live entertainment and gourmet dining.

After a relaxing morning, embark into the Lahbab red dunes. Experience heart-racing 4x4 dune bashing led by expert desert drivers, try thrilling sandboarding down steep dunes, and embark on a camel caravan at sunset. Enjoy an authentic Arabic BBQ feast with belly dancers and Tanoura shows under the night sky.

Ascend the world's tallest building with VIP tickets to the 124th floor observation deck of Burj Khalifa. Witness the choreography of the Dubai Fountain. In the afternoon, cross Dubai Creek on a traditional Abra boat to explore the fragrant Spice Souk and dazzling Gold Souk.

Enjoy a lavish morning buffet and relax by the hotel's rooftop infinity pool. Complete your duty-free shopping and souvenirs before your private chauffeur transfers you smoothly to Dubai International Airport.
